# Contributing

Contributions are welcome through pull requests.

1. Open an issue first for a significant change.
2. Follow the section anatomy in [`01-section/rule-document.md`](01-section/rule-document.md):
   every rule says how it is verified and cites its evidence.
3. A new citation needs a verified record in the
   [engineering casebook](https://github.com/jesusarielgb-works/engineering-casebook) first.
   Never cite a source you have not read.
4. Do not move or rename a file listed in [`anclas.json`](anclas.json); other repositories consume it.
5. Update `CHANGELOG.md` under `[Unreleased]`.
6. All contributions are released under the MIT License.
